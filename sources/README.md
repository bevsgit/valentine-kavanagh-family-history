# Sources

Put the primary sources here so they travel with the research.

Two are referenced throughout the site and are not in the repo yet, because they
are your own files — copy them in:

- `Valentine Dublin Research.xlsx` — the original research spreadsheet, 21 sheets.
  Everything on the site up to 1916 comes from this.
- `1921 Census Scotland - Cumbernauld 495-9-19.pdf` — the ScotlandsPeople page for
  schedule 139, the Wynd, Cumbernauld. This is what added Brendon and Mary, dated
  the emigration, and showed Thomas working on the railway.

Anything else you buy or download — Scottish birth and death certificates, the
1921 census of Scotland for other households, Valuation Rolls — belongs here too.
Name files so the reference is in the filename.

Note that ScotlandsPeople images are Crown copyright and licensed to you
personally. Keeping them in a public repository is a licensing question worth
checking before you push, and is a good reason to keep the spreadsheet and the
certificates here but out of the published `site/` folder.
